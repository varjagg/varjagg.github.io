Roughly speaking there are two groups of people who work on GI problem: career academics in CS or math and ambitious dilletantes. The latter are sometimes also career academics in their own fields. 

* Discovery process

It begun as a side quest off another pet project, right when the pandemic only began to recede. 

I spent a good part of my free time over 3 years to complete the algorithm. It took me half a year to arrive at a variation of Weisfeiler-Leman test. While it was disappointing to discover its limitations, being able to re-trace the steps of two notable algebraists was encouraging. Shortly after that the outline of this algorithm was sketched out and I was excited to run it through with Stig Erik. My dear friend however had his condition worsened by that point to the extent he was transfered to palliative care unit.

I would like also to thank Siarhei Vatlin, who decided it is necessary for us lazy software engineering students to take a detour into abstract algebra. It would have been hard to make even supreficial sense out of some papers in the field otherwise.
